ABOUT THE GAME.

3 AM: Hall of Nightmares is a horror experience built around one unsettling hour: 3:00 AM. Explore abandoned places, discover hidden clues, unlock mysterious doors and uncover the secrets behind every nightmare.

Every nightmare has its own atmosphere and challenge. Explore the Changing House, escape the Abandoned Station and discover the secrets of the Hotel with 13 Floors. Search for keys, investigate strange rooms and find a way out before the nightmare traps you forever.

Explore the unknown

Discover abandoned locations, dark corridors and mysterious places.

Search for clues

Find hidden keys, unlock doors and discover secret rooms.

Escape the nightmare

Solve challenges, explore carefully and try to reach the exit.

Created by Midnight Hollow Studios, this project focuses on atmospheric horror, exploration and mysterious stories. Every detail may hide a clue, and every door may lead somewhere unexpected.
